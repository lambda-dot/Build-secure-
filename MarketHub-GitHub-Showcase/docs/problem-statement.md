# Problem Statement

## PS-02 — MarketHub

Build a multi-vendor marketplace where customers can discover products, manage carts, place orders and track purchases, while vendors can manage their own products and orders.

MarketHub applies the problem to an independent-bookstore marketplace and adds security-focused architecture.

### Functional Scope
- Customer registration/authentication
- Vendor applications and management
- Product management
- Search/filtering
- Cart and wishlist
- Orders and tracking
- Address management
- Simulated checkout
- Reviews
- Social shopping
- Shopping advisor
- Administrative management
- Security signals

### Security Objective

The objective is not to claim absolute security. The project instead aims to reduce attack surface, enforce least privilege, isolate users/vendors, validate input, protect sessions/data, enforce business rules server-side, record security events and continuously validate important security assumptions.
